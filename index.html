      📋 Tracker Appendix
      Everything the tracker does, what each colour means, and why each threshold is set where it is.
      Built by First Bus Franchise · Engineering.
      🔍 Vehicle Inspections — Row Colours
      Green — Due Today
      The inspection is scheduled for today. Acts as a same-day reminder to the workshop.
      Amber — 1–2 Days Overdue
      A short-slip window. Inspections can drift by a day due to resource pressure; amber gives one working day to recover before escalating.
      Red — 3+ Days Overdue
      DVSA require inspections to be completed within their scheduled window. Three or more days overdue is a compliance risk and must be investigated immediately.
      🔧 Servicing — Row Colours
      Green — Due today or within tolerance
      The service is scheduled within the acceptable window (0–14 days past due). Normal scheduling float.
      Amber — 15–34 Days Overdue
      Service is drifting. Major services are resource-intensive and some slippage is expected, but 15+ days warrants active planning.
      Red — 35+ Days Overdue
      Significant overdue. At this point the vehicle's service record is outside any reasonable tolerance and must be escalated to Engineering management.
      ⚠ A / B / C Minor Services
      Pink border + ⚠ Minor Late badge — 7+ Days Overdue, Not Completed
      A, B and C services are minor scheduled maintenance items — oil level checks, filter inspections, coolant and fluid top-ups. They are quick to perform but are the first line of defence against engine wear.

      Standard service thresholds (15/35 days) are too lenient for these short-cycle tasks. Seven days is used because most A/B/C services are scheduled every 4–6 weeks; slipping a week already represents >15 % of the service interval. The pink colour (First Bus brand accent) is chosen to be visually distinct from amber/red rather than suggesting a severity level — it means action soon, not crisis.
      The highlight only appears on rows that are not yet completed.
      🟠 Date Mismatch
      Orange left border — Signed Date ≠ Scheduled Date
      The date the inspection was signed off differs from the date it was scheduled. This can indicate a paperwork error, a late sign-off, or that the vehicle wasn't inspected on the correct day. The Mismatch Report button (orange, top of inspections table) exports all mismatches from the last 30 days for audit purposes.
      🏷 Priority Badges
      VOR
      VOR — Vehicle Off Road
      The vehicle is unavailable (awaiting parts, labour, MOT, etc.). VOR vehicles are automatically excluded from the Missed Only inspection report to prevent false alarms — you can't inspect a vehicle that isn't there.
      OV
      OV — Schedule Override
      A formal reason code explaining why a work order has been deferred. Hover over the badge to see the full description. Override codes ensure all schedule changes are documented and auditable.

      🔎 Filters & Reports
      Missed Only (Inspections) — filters the inspection table to show only overdue rows where the vehicle is not VOR. An Email Report button appears; clicking it drafts a plain-text email you can send straight to the depot team for the daily stand-up.
      Overdue Services — filters the servicing table to show only services more than 14 days past their scheduled date. The 14-day threshold separates normal scheduling float from genuinely overdue work. An Email Report button appears when the filter is active.
      Mismatch Report (orange button) — scans the last 30 days of inspections for signed date vs. scheduled date discrepancies and generates a plain-text audit email. Use this for compliance reviews or DVSA preparation.
      🗂 Depot Sidebar & Fleet Timeline
      Depots panel — lists all depots found in the CSV with a count of active work orders. Clicking a depot filters all tables and the chart to that depot only. All Depots resets the view.
      Fleet panel — appears when a depot is selected. Lists every vehicle in that depot. A red dot next to a fleet number means that vehicle has at least one overdue item. Use the search box to jump straight to a fleet number or registration.
      Bus Timeline — click any fleet number to open a full service and inspection history for that vehicle, ordered newest-first with gap labels between events. Useful for MOT preparation, pre-sale checks, or investigating a vehicle's recent maintenance pattern. Click ← Back to return to the depot view.
      📊 Daily Workload Chart
      Shows how many inspections (pink bars) and services (orange bars) are scheduled on each day across the data range. The red dashed line marks today. Use this to identify busy periods and plan workshop resource in advance — a spike next Tuesday means labour needs to be confirmed now.
      The chart responds to the depot filter — selecting a depot shows workload for that site only.
      📄 CSV Column Guide
      The tracker auto-detects column names — exact headings are flexible as long as they contain these keywords:

      equipment — fleet number depot or location — depot name/code description — work type (drives inspection vs service split) status — current WO status sched / start date — scheduled date signed — date inspection was signed off date completed — date work was completed priority — VOR / OV codes live here
      Rows with no valid scheduled date, no fleet number, or a status of Cancelled / A are automatically excluded from all views.
